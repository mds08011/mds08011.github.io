Notes

Why one-tap timestamps

Handwritten sample times are the weak link in a field tracer study: the tech is holding a bottle, a reagent kit, and a phone, and “about 10:35” gets reconstructed later. Capturing the device timestamp to the second at the grab, and back-filling the residual after titration, keeps the time column trustworthy — which is the column the T10 analysis actually depends on.

Matching against SCADA flow data

Export the CSV and join it against the historian's flow trend on the timestamp column. If the device clock offset was noted at the start, apply it as a constant shift. Sampling on a fixed cadence (e.g. every 2 minutes through the breakthrough curve, stretching out on the tail) makes the join and the curve both cleaner.
