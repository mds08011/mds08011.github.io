How the Numbers Are Derived

The geometry
  • Annular: V = π/4 × (D_casing² − D_carrier²) / 144 × L (cf, diameters in inches)
  • Pipe abandonment: V = π/4 × D_ID² / 144 × L
  • Structure: V = L × W × depth

Conversions: 27 cf/CY, 7.48 gal/cf. A ready-mix or CLSM truck is typically ~10 CY; a grout plant batches by the cf — the gallons line helps size pump strokes and batch counts.

Field notes
  • Carrier buoyancy: an empty carrier pipe in a flooded annulus floats hard against the casing crown during grouting. Fill the carrier with water first, grout in stages, or use restrained spacers per the plan — an air-filled HDPE carrier in fluid grout generates serious uplift per foot.
  • Vents and verification: grout in at the low end, vent at the high end; the job is done when grout returns at the vent, not when the estimate says so. The estimate is for ordering, the vent is for acceptance.
  • Cellular (foamed) grout is common for large annulus and abandonment fills specifically because it's light (less carrier buoyancy differential, less pressure on old pipe) and flows far — yields and densities come from the supplier's design, not a standard table.
  • Abandonment: most specs require the abandoned line be verified full — bulkheads at both ends, fill from the low end, and account for the volume of any laterals or structures on the run that will also take grout.
