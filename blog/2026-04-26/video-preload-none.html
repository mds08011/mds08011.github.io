Stopping HTML5 Videos from Preloading

April 2026

Quick HTML tip I picked up from Simon Willison's TIL site [1]: by default, browsers often start downloading a video as soon as a page loads, even if nobody clicks play. On a slow connection or a page with large clips, that wastes a lot of bandwidth.

Adding preload="none" to the HTML <video> tag tells the browser to hold off on downloading the video until the user clicks play [1].

Put this tag in your HTML page, replacing demonstration.mp4 with your video file path. Adding a poster image is also useful so visitors see a still thumbnail instead of a blank box before hitting play:

<video controls preload="none" poster="thumbnail.jpg" src="demonstration.mp4"></video>

Here controls shows the browser's play/pause bar, src points to the video file, and preload="none" stops the browser from fetching the file ahead of time.

References

  1. Willison, S. "HTML video that loads when the user clicks play". Available: https://til.simonwillison.net/html/video-preload-none.
