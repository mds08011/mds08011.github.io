Working-Day Contract Time Tracker

July 2026

On a working-day construction contract, allowed time is measured in chargeable workdays rather than calendar days. Weekends, specified holidays, and logged non-working days (such as weather delays or owner suspensions) are excluded from the count. This tracker lets you enter the notice-to-proceed (NTP, the formal date contract time begins) date, contract duration in working days, Day 1 convention (whether NTP counts as Day 1 or counting begins on the next working day), holiday toggle, and a dated list of no-work days with notes.

The page steps through each calendar day from the start date to today. A date is charged if it falls Monday through Friday, is not an observed federal holiday (when that checkbox is enabled), and is not in the no-work log. From the contract duration D (working days) and charged days to date C (working days), it computes remaining time R = D − C (working days) and percent used (C / D) × 100. To estimate a projected completion date, it steps forward from today counting future chargeable weekdays until R working days have been used. Because future weather days are unknown, adding a no-work day shifts the projected completion date later.

Rather than storing a fixed list of dates for specific years, the script computes all eleven U.S. federal holidays under 5 U.S.C. § 6103 for any year spanned by the project [1]. Fixed-date holidays (New Year's Day, Juneteenth, Independence Day, Veterans Day, and Christmas Day) use standard observation shifts: a Saturday holiday is observed on Friday, and a Sunday holiday is observed on Monday [1]. Floating holidays are generated from their calendar rules:

MLK Day            3rd Monday in January
Washington's Bday  3rd Monday in February
Memorial Day       last Monday in May
Labor Day          1st Monday in September
Columbus Day       2nd Monday in October
Thanksgiving       4th Thursday in November

All contract settings and no-work log entries are saved in the browser's localStorage (local device storage that stays on your browser between sessions). The page runs offline with no external scripts.

Assumptions and limits
  • The contract's General Conditions (standard administrative clauses) govern the exact definition of a working day, whether NTP is Day 1, and which holidays are excluded. Any state or local holidays not on the federal list can be added manually to the no-work log.
  • The owner's official day count, usually maintained by the resident engineer (RE, the owner's on-site representative) on daily reports, is the contractual record. Comparing counts at each pay application (periodic contractor billing) helps catch differences early.
  • Under most public works contracts, weather makes a day non-chargeable only when it prevents work on the controlling operation (the critical-path activity governing overall completion). Recording what work was delayed in the note field alongside daily reports and photos keeps the log verifiable.
