Replacing the Granny Knot and Supporting Independent Web Resources

2026.07

The Problem

A lot of people tie an unbalanced "Granny Knot" without realizing it. If your shoelace bow naturally twists so the loops point toward your toes and heel instead of sitting straight across the shoe, that's a Granny Knot, and it comes undone easily [1].

Ian's Shoelace Site

Ian Fieggen ("Professor Shoelace") has maintained a hand-coded website about shoelaces for over 20 years. He came up with the "Ian Knot," which is a fast way to tie a standard balanced shoelace bow, and his site walks through it with step-by-step diagrams [2].

Why I Donated

I haven't actually switched to tying the Ian Knot myself, but I still sent him a donation. Independent, obscure reference sites like his are a huge part of why the web is useful, and people regularly copy his diagrams and text without credit. He calls it his "Million Dollar Website" because of the opportunity cost of maintaining it for 20+ years (he estimates he has earned less than 1/5 of the Australian minimum wage for the time he's put in) [3]. His support page takes PayPal donations and also lets donors sponsor individual pages to remove Google ads from them [3].
