Generating QR Codes with Vanilla JavaScript

April 2025

I wanted a simple QR code generator that does not send links or WiFi passwords to a third-party server, so I adapted a local copy of Simon Willison's single-file QR Code Generator [1][2].

Open the page, pick a tab (URL/text or WiFi), fill in the fields, and it draws the QR code locally in your browser using the qrcode-generator JavaScript library (loaded from cdnjs [3]). From there you can copy or download the image. Your WiFi network name (SSID) and password stay in your browser and are never posted to a backend server.

The tool: QR Code Generator.
