Running Tesseract OCR Locally in the Browser

March 2026

OCR (Optical Character Recognition) pulls selectable text out of scanned PDFs and screenshots. Most free OCR websites ask you to upload your document to someone else's server. I adapted a local copy of Simon Willison's Browser OCR tool [1][2] so the whole thing runs inside your browser.

Open the page and pick a PDF or image file, or paste an image straight from your clipboard (Ctrl + V, or Cmd + V on macOS). For PDFs, it uses PDF.js to turn each page into an image first, then runs Tesseract.js (a JavaScript port of the Tesseract OCR engine) locally in your browser to read the text out of it [2]. Simon Willison also wrote a short TIL showing a browser-console Tesseract.js snippet for OCRing images on a webpage [3].
