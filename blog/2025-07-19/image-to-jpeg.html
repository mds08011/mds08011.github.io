Client-Side Image Format Conversion to JPEG

July 2025

This is a local copy of Simon Willison's Image to JPEG Converter [1][2], hosted here so you can convert PNG or WebP images to JPEG right in your browser without uploading files to a web service or installing ImageMagick.

Drop one or more images onto the page (or pick files, or paste from your clipboard), adjust the JPEG quality slider and optional maximum width, and download the converted files. The page uses the browser's built-in HTML5 Canvas (canvas.toDataURL('image/jpeg', quality)) to redraw and compress each image locally, showing the file size before and after. Because it redraws the pixels onto a fresh canvas, any EXIF camera or GPS metadata on the original image is stripped out along the way.

Unlike the original single-image version that resized everything down to 800 pixels wide, this copy handles batches of images and leaves the resolution full-size unless you set a max width.

The tool: Image to JPEG Converter.
