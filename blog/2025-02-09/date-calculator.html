Date Calculator

February 2025

Counting the number of days between two calendar dates by hand is easy to mess up, especially when you only want weekdays or weekend days. I adapted a local copy of Simon Willison's Date Calculator [1][2] so I have a plain, ad-free version hosted on my own site.

To use the Date Calculator, pick a start date and an end date, then choose whether to count all days, business days (Monday through Friday), or weekend days. Everything runs in plain JavaScript in your browser without sending anything to a server.

Note: business days only skip Saturdays and Sundays; the tool does not know about holidays.
