Hydrology and Water/Wastewater Unit Converter

June 2026

This page converts common water, wastewater, and drainage units across five categories: flow rate, volume, rainfall depth over an area, rainfall intensity (rate of rain accumulation over time, such as inches per hour), and land area. Entering one value updates a full table of equivalent units at once, covering cubic feet per second (cfs), gallons per minute (GPM), million gallons per day (MGD), acre-feet per day, liters per second (L/s), cubic meters per second (m³/s), cubic feet (cf), US gallons, acre-feet (ac-ft, the volume of water 1 foot deep over 1 acre), acre-inches (1 inch deep over 1 acre), cubic meters (m³), and related area and rainfall units [1].

For rainfall over an area, the calculator converts depth and acreage into total runoff or detention volume (temporary stormwater storage volume) using V = (d / 12) × A × 43,560, where V is volume in cubic feet (cf), d is rainfall depth in inches, A is area in acres, 12 converts inches to feet, and 43,560 is square feet per acre [1].

A separate section computes chemical or pollutant mass loading in pounds per day (lb/day) from concentration and flow using the standard water and wastewater formula L = C × Q × 8.34, where L is mass loading in lb/day, C is concentration in milligrams per liter (mg/L, equivalent to parts per million by mass in dilute water), Q is flow rate in MGD, and 8.34 is the approximate weight of one US gallon of water in pounds (lb/gal) [2].

Notes and limits
  • Common reference factors: 1 cfs = 448.83 GPM = 0.6463 MGD ≈ 1.983 ac-ft/day, 1 MGD = 694.4 GPM = 1.547 cfs, 1 ac-ft = 43,560 cf = 325,851 US gal, 1 acre-inch = 3,630 cf ≈ 27,154 US gal, 1 cf = 7.4805 US gal, and 1 m³ = 264.17 US gal = 35.315 cf [1].
  • MGD and GPM differ by a factor of 694.4, and acre-feet and acre-inches differ by a factor of 12, so verifying units on plans and pump schedules prevents large scaling errors.
  • Flow rates in cfs often represent instantaneous peak flows in drainage calculations, while MGD often represents daily average flows in treatment plants. Converting the units does not convert a daily average into a peak flow.
  • The 8.34 lb/gal factor assumes freshwater with a specific gravity of 1.0 near standard temperature. Dense sludges or concentrated chemical solutions require adjusting for specific gravity [2].
  • All gallons are US liquid gallons and lengths use the international foot (0.3048 m exactly) [1]. The 2-parts-per-million difference between the international foot and the US survey foot is negligible for tank and basin volumes, though it matters for surveying coordinates (see the Coordinate Converter).
  • The calculator runs offline in the browser and saves recent inputs in localStorage (browser-side local storage).
