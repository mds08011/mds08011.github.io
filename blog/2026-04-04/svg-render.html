Rendering SVGs Locally

April 2026

Sometimes you have an SVG (a vector graphic written in XML text) and need a plain PNG or JPEG image to drop into a slide, email, or app that does not accept SVGs. Instead of opening Inkscape or installing ImageMagick, you can convert it in a browser. I put a local copy of Simon Willison's SVG to JPEG/PNG tool [1][2] on my site.

Paste your SVG markup into the text box (or drop an .svg file), set the width, padding, and background color, and download the PNG or JPEG. Under the hood, the page reads the SVG's viewBox dimensions, draws the SVG onto an HTML5 <canvas>, and exports the result locally via toDataURL() [2].

The tool: SVG to JPEG/PNG.

References

  1. Willison, S. "SVG to JPEG/PNG". Available: https://tools.simonwillison.net/svg-render.
  2. Willison, S. "svg-render.html Source Code" (Apache License 2.0, local copy). Available: https://github.com/simonw/tools/blob/main/svg-render.html.
