Previewing HTML Client-Side

June 2025

When you just want to test a small snippet of HTML without saving a file and refreshing a browser tab over and over, a side-by-side preview page is handy. I adapted a local copy of Simon Willison's HTML Live Preview [1][2] onto my site.

Type or paste HTML into the text box on the left and it renders on the right inside a sandboxed iframe (an isolated mini-page inside the browser). Under the hood, it wraps your HTML in a temporary in-memory Blob URL, waits about 300 ms after you pause typing before updating, and clears old temporary URLs when it refreshes [2]. Nothing is sent to a server.

The tool: HTML Live Preview.

References

  1. Willison, S. "HTML Live Preview". Available: https://tools.simonwillison.net/html-preview.
  2. Willison, S. "html-preview.html Source Code" (Apache License 2.0, local copy). Available: https://github.com/simonw/tools/blob/main/html-preview.html.
