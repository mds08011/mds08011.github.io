Converting Raster Images to Vector Graphics Locally

May 2026

If you want to trace a regular pixel image (like a PNG or JPEG) into a scalable SVG vector graphic without uploading it to an online converter, I put a local copy of Simon Willison's Image to SVG tool [1][2] on my site.

Open the page and drop a JPEG or PNG onto the box (or pick a file). It uses the open-source imagetracerjs JavaScript library [3] to trace the image onto shapes and output an SVG right inside your browser, which you can copy or download.

The tool: Image to SVG.
