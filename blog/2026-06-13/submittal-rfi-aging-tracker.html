Submittal and RFI Aging Tracker

June 2026

This tool tracks open construction submittals (shop drawings, product data sheets, and material samples sent for review) and RFIs (Requests for Information, written questions about plans or specifications) and plots how long they have been waiting in review. Set the review-duration threshold in calendar days at the top (default 30 days), click + Add item to add rows, and enter a reference number, title, type (Submittal, RFI, or Other), date sent, and optional note. Leave the Returned date blank while an item is open, fill it in once the review comes back, remove single rows with the × button, or wipe all saved entries with Clear all data.

For each open item, age is age = today − sent in calendar days, where today is the current local date and sent is the date the item was sent for review. For each returned item, cycle time is cycle = returned − sent in calendar days, where returned is the return date. Open items are grouped into five histogram ranges (0–7, 8–14, 15–30, 31–60, and 60+ calendar days), and any bar whose entire range sits past the threshold is highlighted in red (at 30 days, the 31–60 and 60+ bars). Below the chart, the summary panel reports total open items, open counts by type, median open age in days, the single oldest open item by reference or title and age, open items over the threshold, and average cycle time across returned items.

All entries stay in browser localStorage (local storage on your device). This tracker is only a local working summary. The contractual submittal and RFI log remains the project management platform of record (such as Procore, Kahua, e-Builder, or Autodesk Build), and governing submittal procedures and review periods come from the contract specifications, commonly Division 01, Section 01 33 00 [1].

Assumptions and limits
  • Enter the actual date an item was sent rather than a later date when it was entered into the tracker, or reported ages will be understated.
  • When a submittal is returned for revision and resubmitted, add the next revision (for example, R1 after initial submission R0) as a separate row rather than overwriting the first row so each review cycle is measured separately.
  • An old open item does not automatically mean a delayed review. It may be waiting on a contractor resubmittal or additional backup information.
  • Durations are counted in calendar days and do not account for project holidays or contract rules on what constitutes a complete submittal [1].
